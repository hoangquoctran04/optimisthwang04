Hoang Quoc Tran (Trần Quốc Hoàng)

Undergraduate Researcher in Korean Linguistics & Translation • Hanoi University

I am a senior undergraduate student majoring in Korean Studies (Advanced Program) at the Department of Korean Language, Hanoi University (HANU), and a former exchange student in Korean Language and Literature at Kookmin University, Seoul.

My research lies at the intersection of Korean Linguistics, Korean–Vietnamese Translation Studies, AI & Machine Translation Evaluation, and Second Language Acquisition. Specifically, I investigate how neural machine translation and Large Language Models (LLMs) handle literary title functions, pragmatic nuances, and cross-linguistic untranslatability between Korean and Vietnamese, alongside empirical studies on L1 tonal transfer and Sino-Korean (Hanja) lexical retention among Vietnamese learners.
